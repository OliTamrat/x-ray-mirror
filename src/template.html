X-ray Mirror

Anatomy teaching tool

Learn anatomy on a living body.

Point a camera at a person and a reference skeleton, organs and brain follow them as they move. Tap any part to learn what it does, then test yourself with the quiz.

The first start downloads the tracking models (about 12 MB), so it needs an internet connection.

Offline use

Downloads everything the app needs (about 30 MB) so it works later with no internet. Do this once while connected.

Fit to my head

Auto-fit makes small adjustments to width and jaw length: look straight at the camera with your mouth closed and press it. To fine-tune by hand, pick Solid, set “Real me showing through” to about half, then adjust until the eye sockets sit on your eyes, the teeth on your mouth and the chin on your chin.
